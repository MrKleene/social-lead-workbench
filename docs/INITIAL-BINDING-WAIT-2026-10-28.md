# Initial reply binding transition (offline candidate)

A live bounded workflow reached the exact comment and clicked its reply button once, then stopped at `SELECT/BINDING_EMPTY` before input or submit. This proves the initial binding was empty at that observation. It does not prove that a later observation would have matched, or that rendering delay was the sole cause.

The driver now separates initial binding from all later checks. After its one click, it makes at most 15 read-only observations with at most fourteen 100ms waits. Every observation checks same-page restrictions and the existing exact sender/post/comment/author binding. While the binding is EMPTY, a read-only DOM check also requires the exact visible target, one recognized empty editor, no draft and no ambiguous reply UI. Only exact MATCH completes this phase. Missing/invalid/mismatched bindings, restrictions, draft content, ambiguity, cancellation and journal failure stop. Later MATCH-to-EMPTY changes still stop immediately. No second click, forced focus, DOM/state assignment, weaker parent binding or submit retry is added.

Durable fixed stages `INITIAL_BINDING_WAIT`, `INITIAL_BINDING_MATCHED`, and `INITIAL_BINDING_EMPTY_TIMEOUT` distinguish delayed success from permanent absence. They contain no arbitrary text, tokens or target IDs. Existing journal bounds and write-ahead submit semantics remain. A matched binding is not a submission or receipt.

An explicitly approved recovery of a proven non-submission now automatically follows its one send attempt with the existing owned one-window readback and exact receipt reconciliation. It references the original bounded workflow, collection, model job and original failed attempt. It performs no new collection or model call. UNKNOWN remains locked and cannot be retried. Confirmed readback may close only its own proven resolved pause; rules remain OFF.
